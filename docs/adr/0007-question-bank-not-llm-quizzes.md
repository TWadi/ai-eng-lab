# ADR-0007: Hand-written question bank instead of AI-generated quizzes

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-04

## Context

Quizzes were first generated by an AI API, which costs money and can produce wrong questions.

## Decision

Keep a reviewed question bank (`supabase/quiz-bank/`, 8 questions per lecture), generated into a migration. Each quiz draws 5 at random with shuffled options.

## Consequences

Free and reliable. Coverage grows by writing questions. Retaking can reveal the bank (accepted).
